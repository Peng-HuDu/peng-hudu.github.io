Research

Research expertise

Materials design

Design of pentagonal materials and investigation of their physical properties and potential applications. Design of cluster-based materials using superatomic building blocks for energy applications.

Research expertise

Phonon physics & thermal transport

Phonon transport and intrinsic lattice thermal conductivity of crystalline solids. Thermal rectification in asymmetric nanoscale structures.

Research expertise

Machine learning methods

Fitting machine learning models to obtain interatomic force constants and training neural network potentials for systems of interest.

Current project

Epitaxial moiré superstructures

Investigation of Practical Epitaxial Moiré Superstructures with Novel Electronic Properties.

Planned research directions

AI for electromechanical materials

Contribute to the collaborative project “AI for Discovering Materials with Outstanding Electromechanical Response” by developing first-principles-driven AI approaches to identify new material compositions and microstructures.

Machine-learning interatomic potentials

Develop comprehensive datasets and a transferable machine-learning interatomic potential for two-dimensional van der Waals heterostructures, supporting atomistic simulations and subsequent investigations of physical properties.

Doctoral research projects

Na₃OBH₄ superionic conductor

Thermal conductivity of the superatom-based superionic conductor Na₃OBH₄, consisting of the superhalogen cluster BH₄.

Completely superatomic PH₄AlBr₄ crystal

Thermal conductivity of the completely superatomic crystal PH₄AlBr₄, consisting of both superalkali PH₄ and superhalogen AlBr₄.

Cluster-assembled polyhedrons

Thermal rectification in cluster-assembled polyhedrons comprising T₃-type metal chalcogenide supertetrahedral clusters.

Cluster-assembled fractals

Thermal rectification in cluster-assembled fractals from T₂-type metal chalcogenide supertetrahedral clusters.
