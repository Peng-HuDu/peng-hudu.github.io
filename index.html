Research Fellow · National University of Singapore

Computational materials physics

I am a Research Fellow in the Department of Physics at the National University of Singapore. My research background spans the theoretical design of cluster-assembled materials and the computational modeling of thermal transport using multiscale methods.

I received my PhD in Materials Physics and Chemistry from Peking University in July 2025 and my BSc in Materials Physics from Nanjing University in July 2020.

At NUS, I work on the project “Investigation of Practical Epitaxial Moiré Superstructures with Novel Electronic Properties”.
